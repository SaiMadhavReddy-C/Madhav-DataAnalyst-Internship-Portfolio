I’m happy to share my Data Analytics internship journey with ApexPlanet Software Pvt. Ltd.

Across the internship, I worked through an end-to-end analytics project using a sales dataset: data cleaning and quality assessment, exploratory analysis, SQL business questions, KPI design, dashboarding, and statistical validation.

Some of the work I completed included:
• Cleaning missing and inconsistent data
• Building a data dictionary and reusable Python/Pandas workflow
• Exploring sales trends by month, category, product, city, gender, and age group
• Defining business KPIs and creating dashboard views
• Performing statistical hypothesis testing and interpreting the result
• Organizing the work into a professional GitHub portfolio

The biggest takeaway for me was learning that good analytics is not only about creating charts. It starts with trustworthy data, continues with the right business questions, and ends with a clear story that someone can act on.

I’m grateful for the opportunity to strengthen my practical skills and build an end-to-end project that I can continue improving.
