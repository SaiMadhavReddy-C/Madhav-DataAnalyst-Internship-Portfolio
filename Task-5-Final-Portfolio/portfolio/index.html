STATISTICAL VALIDATION

Hypothesis Testing

Testing whether average transaction sales differed between female and male transactions.


Business Question

Is there statistical evidence that the mean transaction sales for female and male transactions are different?

NULL HYPOTHESIS H₀

Female and male mean transaction sales are equal.

ALTERNATIVE H₁

Female and male mean transaction sales are different.

STATISTICAL TEST

Welch's t-test

SIGNIFICANCE LEVEL

α = 0.05

P-VALUE

0.495011

95% CONFIDENCE INTERVAL

[-₹19,079.85, ₹9,231.58]

Fail to Reject H₀ at α = 0.05

The p-value is greater than the 0.05 significance level, so the analysis does not provide sufficient statistical evidence to conclude that the two group means differ. This is a statistical finding for this dataset and should not be interpreted as proof that gender has no effect on purchasing behavior.
